AWS Account Management lets you programmatically manage the settings of your AWS accounts.
This client lets you view and update the account name, the primary and alternate (billing, operations, security) contacts and the primary email address, and enable or disable opt-in Regions, for your own account or for the member accounts of an AWS Organizations organization.
